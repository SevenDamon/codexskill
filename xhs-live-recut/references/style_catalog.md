# Content Style Catalog

Use this catalog to choose narrative structure by conversion goal and source evidence. It does not replace the visual preset in `project-profile.yaml`.

## knowledge-breakdown

Best for paid replay or product links where trust matters.

Structure: misconception → reframe → three-part framework → concrete screenshot/example → CTA.

Risk: feels flat when the hook lacks conflict.

Example hook: “很多老师做 AI 课堂互动，第一步就错了。”

## critical-hook

Best for a stronger Xiaohongshu opening.

Structure: common wrong approach → why it fails → better mental model → one actionable check → CTA.

Risk: can sound arrogant; criticize the method, not the teacher.

Example hook: “你以为是在做网页，其实你缺的是反馈链。”

## before-after

Best when the source contains a clear screen demo, dashboard, student page, or generated result.

Structure: before → after → two or three bridge steps → CTA.

Risk: fails when screenshots are unclear or the states are not genuinely comparable.

Example hook: “学生交了答案，老师还是不知道下一步讲什么？”

## case-review

Best for long-term creator authority.

Structure: real case → unexpected lesson → workflow change → one decision explained → CTA.

Risk: lower immediate click-through; stronger trust value.

Example hook: “这个 AI 课堂案例，最值钱的不是网页。”

## teacher-inner-os

Best for lighter posts and account personality.

Structure: teacher pain in spoken language → emotional recognition → practical AI workflow → CTA.

Risk: overuse weakens professional credibility.

Example hook: “上课最怕的不是学生答错，是你根本不知道谁错在哪里。”

## fast-hook

Best for 20–30 second clips with one simple idea.

Structure: 0–3s hook → 3–12s visual proof → 12–23s two or three checks → 23–30s CTA.

Risk: complex educational workflows lose necessary context.

Example hook: “一个 AI 课堂互动，不应该只做给学生看。”

## Product-link CTA patterns

Prefer specific continuation promises:

- “照着做的完整过程，放在下面的课程回放里。”
- “学生端、收集表、教师分析页怎么连起来，完整搭建过程在课程回放里。”
- “这条只讲判断，完整操作步骤看下面挂的回放。”
- “想直接复刻这个课堂案例，可以看下面的课程回放。”

Avoid generic or defensive lines:

- “完整搭建流程，我不在这条讲完。”
- “想了解更多请点击链接。”
- “欢迎购买我的课程。”
